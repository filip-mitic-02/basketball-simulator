Privacy policy

Perfect Season is a basketball drafting and coaching game. This page explains what the game stores and what third parties receive. Last updated: 2026-11-28.

What we store

If you play as a guest, nothing is stored on our servers. If you sign in with Google, we store: an internal account id, the Google account identifier (a random string, not your email), your first name and the first letter of your family name, and your game data: finished seasons, scores, achievements, experience points, coins and the cosmetics you own. We do not receive or store your email address, photo or contact list.

Why

To save your progress, show a leaderboard, verify results and let you keep your cosmetics across devices.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob (an interstitial after a finished season and optional rewarded videos). AdMob may use the advertising id of your device and similar data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in your device settings. The website version shows no third-party ads.

Cookies

When you sign in, one essential, HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in. We use no analytics or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Google (sign-in), our hosting provider and database provider process data on our behalf to run the game. Your first name, family initial and best scores can appear on the public leaderboard.

Deleting your data

Inside the game, sign in, open "Rules" and use "Delete my account and data". This removes your account, seasons, progress and purchases from our database immediately.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
